Statutory Consumer Rights in India

Legal Metrology Act, 2009 & Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Right against Dual MRP & Price Alterations (Rule 18)

No vendor, restaurant, or railway canteen is permitted to sell any pre-packaged good at a price higher than the factory printed MRP.

Right to Unit Sale Price (USP) Transparency

All items above 100g or 100ml must display price per gram or ml to allow fair comparison without tricking consumers through shrinkflation.

Right to Full Manufacturer Traceability

Complete physical factory address, domestic importer license, and consumer grievance telephone are statutory mandates.
